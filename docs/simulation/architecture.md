# Deterministic Simulator Architecture

SIM-1 is an offline research pipeline. The frozen strategy contract defines trading intent, selection bounds, sizing, policies, exits, and risk. A separate strict simulation-market contract defines the session clock, synthetic option-chain construction, strike grid, eligible expiries, liquidity, option and futures multipliers, futures identity, and futures delta per contract. Both contracts are persisted and hashed.

The underlying path is exogenous state only: UTC timestamp, session identity, spot, realized step fraction, rates, dividend yield, and implied volatility. Its `path_hash` proves that sequence. The executable-state builder validates every point against the configured clock and derives local time, selected-option maturity, futures maturity, and carry price. The distinct `executable_market_state_hash` proves the exact sequence persisted in `market-states.csv`. Changing selection or a futures expiry buffer changes executable-state identity without mutating the underlying path.

Release identity is layered. Path `generator_version` identifies the exogenous generation algorithm and payload generation, while `path_hash` identifies exact path inputs and points. `executable_market_state_hash` identifies the exact derived scenario. `simulator_version` identifies engine behavior. Strategy, simulation-market, and simulation-run `schema_version` fields independently identify their payload shapes, and `manifest_schema_version` identifies the manifest shape. None is interchangeable with another.

The clock interprets decisions in `Asia/Kolkata`, stores timezone-aware local and UTC timestamps, and derives expiry date and time-to-expiry from the same ordered sessions. Its current calendar is weekday-only and does not model NSE holidays or special sessions. Eligible expiries are generated in trading sessions and filtered by STRAT-1 bounds before the earliest eligible expiry is selected. Weekend, shifted-time, non-UTC, and incompatible step-fraction paths fail before execution.

At entry the engine gates premium, synthetic spread, volume, open interest, matched-straddle integrity, and absolute daily theta before journaling fills. Expected edge is recorded as deferred to EDGE-1. At a timestamp the engine marks instruments, recalculates option values and Greeks, aggregates portfolio delta, evaluates kill-switch, loss, hedge-count, expiry-buffer, holding-period, and terminal exits, and only then invokes a hedge policy. It predicts post-policy delta and substitutes a risk-reducing hedge when the hard cap would otherwise be exceeded. An unsatisfiable residual exits deterministically without a non-reducing fill. No future state is supplied to a policy and no exit timestamp opens a routine hedge.

The exact `Decimal` ledger and position lots are accounting truth. Black–Scholes values and beginning-of-interval Greek attribution are analytical views. Reconciliation compares terminal NAV with gross option P&L, gross futures P&L, financing, and separately accumulated costs.

There are no HTTP, WebSocket, broker, database, Redis, scheduler, or frontend boundaries. The CLI is the only run entry point.
